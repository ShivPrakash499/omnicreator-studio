### Beat 3: HUD Formula Vault & Memory Hack (Snell's Law, Refractive Index & Lens Formula Vault)
- **Formula:** `n = sin(i) / sin(r) = c / v  |  1/f = 1/v - 1/u`
- **SI Units:** Refractive Index (n): Unitless | Focal length (f), u, v: meter (m) | Power P = 1/f(m) in Dioptre (D)
- **Sign Conventions:** Cartesian Sign Rule: Incident light = +X. Object distance (u) is ALWAYS Negative (-). Concave f = (-), Convex f = (+).
- **Sticky Hinglish Mnemonic:** *LENS vs MIRROR Hack: 'Lens mein MINUS (1/v - 1/u), Magnification mein PLUS (v/u)' — ulta mat karna!*
